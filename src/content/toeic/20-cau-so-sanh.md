## 2. CÁC TRƯỜNG HỢP ĐẶC BIỆT CẦN NHỚ

<span class="cs-badge rose">Hay bị bẫy nhất</span>

Có **3 nhóm ngoại lệ** mà đề thi Part 5 rất hay dùng để đánh lừa thí sinh:

### Nhóm 1: Tính từ tận cùng bằng "-y" (Dù 2 vần vẫn coi là ngắn!)

<div class="cs-box info">
  Tính từ kết thúc bằng <strong>-y</strong> → Đổi <strong>y → i</strong> rồi thêm <strong>-er / -est</strong> (KHÔNG dùng <em>more / most</em>)<br/>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;• Happy → <strong>Happier / The Happiest</strong><br/>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;• Easy → <strong>Easier / The Easiest</strong><br/>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;• Early → <strong>Earlier / The Earliest</strong><br/>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;• Heavy → <strong>Heavier / The Heaviest</strong>
</div>

### Nhóm 2: Bảng tính từ bất quy tắc

## 4. CAU TRUC SO SANH DAC BIET TRONG TOEIC

<span class="cs-badge violet">Bay diem cao 700+</span>

### Cau truc "The + So Sanh Nhat + S + V + Ever"

<div class="cs-formula">
  <span>the</span>
  <span class="sep">+</span>
  <span>Adj-EST / most + Adj</span>
  <span class="sep">+</span>
  <span>S + have/has ever + V3</span>
</div>

> *This is **the most productive quarter** our company has ever had.*
> *(Day la quy co nang suat cao nhat ma cong ty chung toi tung dat duoc.)*

### Cau truc Song Song "The more... the more..." (Cang ... cang ...)

<div class="cs-formula">
  <span>The + So sanh hon</span>
  <span class="sep">,</span>
  <span>the + So sanh hon</span>
</div>

> *The **harder** you work, the **luckier** you get.*
> *(Ban lam viec cang cham chi, ban cang gap may man.)*

<div class="cs-box warn">
  <strong>Bay thuong gap:</strong> Ca 2 ve deu phai dung dang So sanh hon. Khong the dung nguyen the (hard, lucky) hay so sanh nhat (hardest, luckiest) trong cau truc nay!
</div>

### Cau truc "As + Adj + as possible" (Cang ... cang tot)

> *Please respond **as soon as possible.*** (Viet tat **ASAP** trong van phong.)
> *The report should be **as detailed as possible.***

### So sanh phu dinh "not as ... as" = "less ... than"

<div class="cs-box info">
  Hai cau truc sau co y nghia <strong>tuong duong nhau</strong>:<br/>
  • This plan is <strong>not as efficient as</strong> the previous one.<br/>
  • This plan is <strong>less efficient than</strong> the previous one.<br/>
  <em>(Ke hoach nay kem hieu qua hon ke hoach truoc.)</em>
</div>

## 5. BAY THUONG GAP VA CACH PHAN BIET

<span class="cs-badge amber">Tranh mat diem oan</span>

<div class="cs-step-card">
  <div class="cs-step-header"><span class="cs-step-num">1</span> Bay 1: Dung "more" voi tinh tu ngan</div>
  <div>
    ❌ <em>This laptop is <del>more fast</del> than that one.</em><br/>
    ✅ <em>This laptop is <strong>faster</strong> than that one.</em><br/>
    <strong>Nguyen tac:</strong> Tinh tu 1 van → Bat buoc dung duoi <strong>-er</strong>, khong dung "more".
  </div>
</div>

<div class="cs-step-card">
  <div class="cs-step-header"><span class="cs-step-num">2</span> Bay 2: Thieu "the" trong so sanh nhat</div>
  <div>
    ❌ <em>She is <del>most talented</del> employee in the office.</em><br/>
    ✅ <em>She is <strong>the most talented</strong> employee in the office.</em><br/>
    <strong>Nguyen tac:</strong> So sanh nhat luon phai co <strong>"the"</strong> dung truoc.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="cs-step-card">
  <div class="cs-step-header"><span class="cs-step-num">3</span> Bay 3: Nham "elder" va "older"</div>
  <div>
    • <strong>Elder</strong>: Chi dung trong gia dinh (my <em>elder</em> brother/sister) — KHONG dung voi <em>than</em>.<br/>
    • <strong>Older</strong>: Dung chung cho nguoi va vat — CO dung voi <em>than</em>.<br/>
    ✅ <em>My brother is <strong>older than</strong> me by 5 years.</em>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="cs-step-card">
  <div class="cs-step-header"><span class="cs-step-num">4</span> Bay 4: "Superior / Inferior / Senior / Junior" khong dung "than"</div>
  <div>
    Cac tinh tu goc Latin sau day dung gioi tu <strong>"to"</strong> thay vi "than":<br/>
    <span class="pill violet">superior to</span>
    <span class="pill violet">inferior to</span>
    <span class="pill violet">senior to</span>
    <span class="pill violet">junior to</span>
    <span class="pill violet">prior to</span><br/><br/>
    ✅ <em>This product is <strong>superior to</strong> its competitors.</em> (KHONG phai "superior <del>than</del>"!)
  </div>
</div>

## Bai Tap Thuc Hanh TOEIC PART 5 (Co Dap An Chi Tiet)

A. efficient  
B. more efficient than  
C. the most efficient  
D. as efficient  

**Cau 2:** Of all the candidates interviewed, Ms. Nguyen was considered __________.  
A. more qualified  
B. as qualified as  
C. the most qualified  
D. qualified than  

**Cau 3:** The company's quarterly profits were __________ analysts had initially projected.  
A. significant higher than  
B. significantly higher than  
C. the highest than  
D. more high than  

**Cau 4:** The production process has become __________ efficient over the past few months.  
A. much more  
B. much most  
C. more most  
D. as much  

**Cau 5:** Our new delivery service is __________ to what most competitors currently offer in the market.  
A. better  
B. superior  
C. more superior  
D. more better  

**Cau 6:** The __________ Ms. Park advances in her career, the __________ opportunities she discovers.  
A. further — many more  
B. more further — more  
C. farther — much  
D. more — more

### Dap An Va Giai Thich Chi Tiet

<div class="cs-box success">
  <strong>1. Dap an: B (more efficient than)</strong><br/>
  • <em>Tin hieu:</em> Phia sau cho trong co <code>the old system</code> (cum danh tu) → Day la cau truc <strong>So sanh Hon</strong>.<br/>
  • "Efficient" la tinh tu 3 van → Dung <strong>more + Adj + than</strong>.<br/>
  • <em>Dich:</em> Phan mem ke toan moi hieu qua hon he thong cu ma chung toi dung nam ngoai.
</div>

<div class="cs-box success">
  <strong>2. Dap an: C (the most qualified)</strong><br/>
  • <em>Tin hieu:</em> Phia truoc co <code>Of all the candidates</code> → Tin hieu 100% cua <strong>So sanh Nhat</strong>.<br/>
  • "Qualified" la tinh tu dai → Dung <strong>the most + Adj</strong>.<br/>
  • <em>Dich:</em> Trong so tat ca ung vien duoc phong van, co Nguyen duoc coi la nguoi co nang luc nhat.
</div>

<div class="cs-box success">
  <strong>3. Dap an: B (significantly higher than)</strong><br/>
  • <em>Tin hieu:</em> Co "than" o cuoi → Can <strong>So sanh Hon</strong>. Phia truoc co trang tu "significantly" (nhan manh) → Xac nhan dung <strong>So sanh Hon</strong>.<br/>
  • A sai vi "significant" (tinh tu) khong the bo nghia cho "higher" — phai dung trang tu <strong>significantly</strong>.<br/>
  • <em>Dich:</em> Loi nhuan hang quy cua cong ty cao hon dang ke so voi du doan ban dau cua cac nha phan tich.
</div>

<div class="cs-box success">
  <strong>4. Dap an: A (much more)</strong><br/>
  • Cau truc can dien: <code>become ___ efficient</code> → Can trang tu nhan manh + so sanh hon.<br/>
  • <strong>Much more efficient</strong> = hieu qua hon nhieu (much nhan manh so sanh hon).<br/>
  • B, C sai cau truc; D sai ngu nghia.<br/>
  • <em>Dich:</em> Quy trinh san xuat da tro nen hieu qua hon nhieu trong vai thang qua.
</div>

<div class="cs-box success">
  <strong>5. Dap an: B (superior)</strong><br/>
  • <em>Tin hieu:</em> Phia sau co gioi tu <code>to</code> → Day la dau hieu nhan biet tinh tu goc Latin: <strong>superior to</strong> (khong dung "than").<br/>
  • C sai vi "superior" da mang nghia so sanh, khong them "more".<br/>
  • <em>Dich:</em> Dich vu giao hang moi cua chung toi vuot troi hon so voi nhung gi hau het cac doi thu canh tranh hien dang cung cap tren thi truong.
</div>

<div class="cs-box success">
  <strong>6. Dap an: D (more — more)</strong><br/>
  • Cau truc <strong>"The + So sanh hon..., the + So sanh hon..."</strong> (Cang ... cang ...).<br/>
  • <code>more ... more opportunities</code> → Ca 2 ve deu can dang <strong>So sanh Hon</strong>.<br/>
  • A sai vi "many more" khong dung trong cau truc nay; B va C sai dang so sanh.<br/>
  • <em>Dich:</em> Co Park cang thang tien trong su nghiep, co cang kham pha ra nhieu co hoi hon.
</div>
