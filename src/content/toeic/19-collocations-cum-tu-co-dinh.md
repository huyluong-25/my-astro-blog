## 7. Bài Tập Luyện Nhanh (10 Câu TOEIC-Style)

Chọn đáp án đúng nhất điền vào chỗ trống:

**1.** The committee will ___ a decision about the new policy next week.
> A. do &nbsp;&nbsp; **B. make ✓** &nbsp;&nbsp; C. take &nbsp;&nbsp; D. get

**2.** All employees are required to ___ the mandatory safety training.
> A. join &nbsp;&nbsp; B. take part &nbsp;&nbsp; **C. attend ✓** &nbsp;&nbsp; D. participate

**3.** ___ the recent merger, the two companies will operate as one entity.
> A. Despite &nbsp;&nbsp; **B. As a result of ✓** &nbsp;&nbsp; C. Even though &nbsp;&nbsp; D. Unless

**4.** The contract is ___ approval from the legal department.
> A. regardless of &nbsp;&nbsp; **B. subject to ✓** &nbsp;&nbsp; C. due to &nbsp;&nbsp; D. in case of

**5.** Please ___ the application form and submit it by Friday.
> **A. fill out ✓** &nbsp;&nbsp; B. carry out &nbsp;&nbsp; C. set up &nbsp;&nbsp; D. draw up

**6.** I will ___ you with the updated schedule as soon as possible.
> A. look into &nbsp;&nbsp; B. come up with &nbsp;&nbsp; **C. get back to ✓** &nbsp;&nbsp; D. point out

**7.** The annual conference will be ___ in the downtown convention center.
> A. done &nbsp;&nbsp; B. made &nbsp;&nbsp; **C. held ✓** &nbsp;&nbsp; D. taken

**8.** Employees with more than 5 years of experience are ___ for an additional bonus.
> A. responsible &nbsp;&nbsp; B. qualified &nbsp;&nbsp; C. capable &nbsp;&nbsp; **D. eligible ✓**

**9.** The manager ___ several issues with the new system during the inspection.
> A. looked into &nbsp;&nbsp; B. carried out &nbsp;&nbsp; **C. pointed out ✓** &nbsp;&nbsp; D. dealt with

**10.** ___ your recent inquiry, we are pleased to provide the following information.
> A. In terms of &nbsp;&nbsp; B. Due to &nbsp;&nbsp; **C. With regard to ✓** &nbsp;&nbsp; D. In spite of

<div class="tip-box">

**💡 Chiến thuật học collocations hiệu quả:**

1. **Học theo cặp**, không học đơn lẻ: đừng học "make" riêng — hãy học "make a decision", "make a reservation", "make a profit" cùng nhau.
2. **Gặp collocation lạ trong đề → ghi chú ngay** vào danh sách cá nhân.
3. **Luyện Part 6 nhiều** — collocations xuất hiện cực kỳ dày đặc trong phần điền vào đoạn văn.
